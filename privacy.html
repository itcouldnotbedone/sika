Sika privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026.

Who uses Sika

Sika is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channel @ItCouldNotBeDone, who signs in with their own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through YouTube API Services, Sika reads that channel's own data: its videos and their details, live streams and their chat, YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data. It can upload and update videos on that channel when the owner asks it to. It does not access any other channel or any other person's account.

How the data is stored and used

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to help the owner understand and improve their own videos and streams. It is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. After access is revoked, Sika stops collecting, and data obtained through YouTube API Services is deleted within 7 days, as YouTube's API policies require.

Google and YouTube

Sika uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
